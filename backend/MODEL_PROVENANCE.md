**Important operational constraint:** XGBoost model is bypassed for trips < 50 km.
See `predict_travel_time` in `app/main.py`. Testing showed −21.6 min output for
150 km inputs — the model extrapolates outside its training distribution for
urban commute distances.

**Mode-encoding limitation at highway distances:** The training data was dominantly
car/driving trips. The `mode_enc` feature is present but the model produces
implausible implied speeds for motorcycle modes at highway distances: boda_boda
101 km/h over 300 km, okada 85 km/h over 500 km (confirmed from reference test
cases). These values are never reached by typical urban commutes (all < 50 km →
formula path). Documented as out-of-distribution behaviour, not a serialisation bug.
